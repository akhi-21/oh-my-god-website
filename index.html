Scene 01 // The Studio

Cinematic storytelling with cultural depth.

Oh My God Pictures was founded with one singular mission: to create films that leave an indelible mark on the collective consciousness of audiences worldwide. Built in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, the studio approaches every production as a crafted experience where visual poetry, emotional honesty, and production discipline exist in perfect balance.

Our portfolio embraces stories that feel timeless yet relevant, rooted in heritage while reaching toward a modern audience. From ad films and music videos to cinematic narratives designed for regional and national reach, we believe in creating work that is elegant, purposeful, and impossible to ignore.

Every project is developed with an eye for detail, a respect for collaboration, and a commitment to delivering premium production value from concept to final frame. We do not simply produce visuals. We create memorable screen experiences.

Scene 02 // The Visionary

Dr. Nagaraj Vishnu

Founder Chairman

Dr. Nagaraj Vishnu stands at the intersection of public service, cultural identity, and forward-looking creative leadership. His journey reflects a rare combination of institutional insight, entrepreneurial conviction, and a deep respect for community-led progress.

With a professional path shaped by governance, accountability, business leadership, and political participation, he has built a reputation for turning vision into impact. That same clarity now drives Oh My God Pictures, where he champions stories that are socially resonant, visually refined, and rooted in purpose.

As Founder Chairman, he guides the company with an emphasis on legacy, innovation, and meaningful contribution. His outlook is not limited to entertainment alone; it extends to how storytelling can preserve identity, inspire aspiration, and create a lasting cultural footprint for audiences in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, and beyond.

A Legacy of Contribution

Heritage that informs every frame.

The founder's story is closely associated with a legacy of contribution connected to the Madras High Court, reflecting a generational respect for justice, discipline, and public responsibility. This heritage brings a sense of gravitas to the company’s creative direction and long-term vision.

Equally important is a strong Arya Vysya community identity that values enterprise, integrity, and service. Together, these roots shape a production house that sees cinema not only as an artistic pursuit, but also as a vehicle for preserving values, honoring community, and building a meaningful legacy.
